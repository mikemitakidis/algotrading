## What this does and does not show

- **Does:** the lab labels like M18. Its features and folds cannot see the future, and both audits are shown to catch leaks. Under the null it does not invent improvements, at the measured rate and bound. When non-linear or regime structure is planted, it finds it at the measured power.
- **Does not:** say anything about real markets. A model that wins on these synthetic benchmarks has shown only that the machinery works. Real-data use needs approved data (lane F), a pre-registration (lane B) and the forward-test ladder.
