## 2. Data-snooping tests across 20 null and 20 planted (strength 0.4) markets

| datasets | Hansen SPA rejections at 5 % (95 % CI) | White RC rejections at 5 % (95 % CI) | mean PBO | mean effective trials (eigen / cluster) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| null | 1/20 (0–25 %) | 0/20 (0–17 %) | 0.47 | 3.4 / 3.6 |
| planted_0.4 | 20/20 (83–100 %) | 20/20 (83–100 %) | 0.27 | 2.8 / 2.2 |

Mean PBO is 0.47 on null markets (about 0.5 means picking the in-sample best variant is pure noise) and 0.27 on planted markets. PBO diagnoses the *selection among variants*; when every variant shares one effect their ranking is still partly noise, so PBO alone is not an edge test. The 12 variants behave like about 3.4 independent trials (eigenvalue estimate), which is what a deflated Sharpe should be charged for if trials are correlated.
