## 4. Ledgered parameter search: surface, plateau, deflation

## What this does and does not show

- **Does:** the research engine reproduces M17 exactly where their semantics overlap, and extends it to portfolios with explicit, tested capacity, exposure, cash, membership, delisting, time and trailing rules. Templates are causal, and each is measured against the effect it targets. The search charges for every trial and reports surfaces, not just peaks.
- **Does not:** show that any template works on real markets. Synthetic markets are long-biased (positive drift in the calm regime), so absolute returns mostly reflect the market, not the template; only the paired differences isolate the planted effects. Real-data evaluation needs approved data (lane F), pre-registration (lane B) and the forward-test ladder.
